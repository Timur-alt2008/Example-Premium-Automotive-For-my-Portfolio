How we do it

A ceramic coating is a layer of silicon dioxide about 1–2 µm thick that bonds chemically with the clear coat. It is harder than factory lacquer, repels water and dirt, and resists car-care chemicals.

We apply the coating in a separate bay with controlled temperature and humidity, then leave the car overnight under infrared lamps to cure.
