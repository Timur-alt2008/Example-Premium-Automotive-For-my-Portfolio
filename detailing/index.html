About “Multi-stage polishing”

How a job runsin the studio

From request to handover in five steps. At each one you get photos and know exactly what is happening with your car.

  1. 01

    Request

    You describe the job, we suggest a time and an estimated price.

  2. 02

    Inspection under light

    We measure the paint thickness, photograph defects and agree the scope of work.

  3. 03

    Work

    One specialist handles the car, and you get photos of every stage.

  4. 04

    Inspection

    The studio manager checks every panel under three kinds of light.

  5. 05

    Handover

    We show you the result, explain aftercare and hand over the coating certificate.
