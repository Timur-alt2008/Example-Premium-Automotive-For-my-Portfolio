A workshopfull of light

Example grew out of a simple idea: a car only looks truly cared for if it passes inspection under any light. Everything else follows from that.

How we work

Light first, then the work

Every car starts with an inspection under three kinds of light and a paint thickness reading. We show you the defects before we quote a price, so you can see exactly what you pay for.

One specialist, one car

The car is never passed between shifts. The specialist who checked it in hands it back to you and can tell you about every panel.

The rental fleet — by the same rules

The collection is looked after by the same people who work on our clients’ cars. Before every handover: a wash, an inspection under the lights and an interior check.

Why the carscome back to us

Seven rules that never change — whether it is a rental McLaren or your family SUV.

  • Professional equipment

    Dual-action polishers with 15 and 21 mm throws, paint thickness gauges, infrared curing and a plotter for cutting film.

  • Products with data sheets

    We only use products that come with a technical data sheet and a clear service life.

  • One specialist per car

    One specialist handles the car from check-in to handover — and knows every panel of it.

  • Inspection under three kinds of light

    Diffused light, a spot torch and full-spectrum sunlight. The work is finished only when nothing shows under any of them.

  • Attention to detail

    Door shuts, badges, drains and the inside of the wheels — all part of the standard job.

  • Privacy

    A gated entrance, a private handover area and no photos of your car without your consent.

  • Comfortable waiting

    A lounge overlooking the bay, coffee and a place to work. Or delivery of the car to your door.
