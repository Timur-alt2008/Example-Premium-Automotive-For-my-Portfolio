Rentalterms

Demonstration terms, written the way a real rental company would write them.

Main requirements

  • Driver aged 23–30+ (depends on the car) with at least 5 years’ experience
  • The deposit is held on the driver’s card and released within 3 working days
  • The car is handed over with a full tank and returned the same way
  • Daily mileage limit; excess mileage — €6 per kilometre
  • Track days, drifting and letting third parties drive are not allowed

Booking and payment

A request on the website is not a booking. A manager confirms availability and terms by phone; payment and the deposit hold happen at handover.

Changes and cancellation

Changing dates and cancelling are free more than 48 hours before the rental starts. Later cancellations are charged one day’s rental.

Insurance

All cars carry comprehensive insurance with the excess stated in the contract. Extended cover reduces the excess to €2,500.

Liability

Traffic fines, toll roads and parking during the rental are paid by the renter. Damage not covered by insurance is deducted from the deposit based on an independent assessment.
